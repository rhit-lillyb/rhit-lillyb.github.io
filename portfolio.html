Portfolio

The Wilds - Operation Catapult

Overview

Operation Catapult was the first time I ever really finished one of my projects due to the two week time limit placed on us. This game, made in PyGame, is a simple survival game where you go around chopping trees and mining stone in a forest. Once you get those resources, you can use them to make four houses in the central plots. The goal is to finish all four houses, and once you do that it'll send you to the end screen. The project link is here.

Personal Details

This project is incredibly important to me, due to the reactions of the people and kids we let playtest. I got to see people experience something I made and feel joy when they reached that end screen. Something about that really set me on the path I'm on now.

High School Projects

One-Player Uno

Overview

This small game was made in JavaScript and lets you play a virtual version of UNO where getting too many cards is game over. It's mostly luck based, but a good stroke of luck can feel very rewarding. The most important thing to me about this project is the fact all the graphics are dynamically drawn and not made of .png or .jpeg images.

Personal Details

This one is special to me, as it won 4th place in a national contest held by Carnegie Mellon University!

Neon Pong

Overview

It's pong! There isn't much to explain aside from the fact this was one of my first full projects in High School. This was also made in JavaScript!

Personal Details

One of my first experiments, it's very funny as instead of programming an AI I made the other paddle just track the ball's y-position. Due to that, the only way to win is to hit the ball so many times it becomes so fast it speeds THROUGH the hitbox of the other paddle. Unorthodox, but at least I found a way to make it work haha.
